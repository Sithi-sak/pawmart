  • Headings are always serif at weight 500; never bold the serif above 600, it muddies at small sizes.
  • Weight 600 is the workhorse emphasis weight in Inter; 700 is reserved for prices and totals that need to win.
  • Uppercase text always carries letter-spacing (0.05em–0.1em); lowercase text never does.
  • Corners are square site-wide, so typography carries most of the visual rhythm; keep the scale steps distinct.
